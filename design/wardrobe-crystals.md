## Modern Wardrobe: mix and match

The dino wears **one item in each of 4 spots at the same time**: head, eyes, neck and back. Today it can wear only one item in total. Items are bought once with Dino Eggs and kept forever. Tap an item to put it on, and tap again to take it off.

| Spot | Items (🆕 = new) | Cost (eggs) |
|---|---|---|
| **Head** | Explorer Hat · 🆕 Cool Cap · 🆕 Headphones · 🆕 Comfy Beanie · 🆕 Flower Crown · Olympiad Crown (Champion and up) | 2–6 |
| **Eyes** | Cool Glasses · 🆕 Star Shades · 🆕 Heart Glasses · 🆕 Smart Goggles | 4–5 |
| **Neck** | Cosy Scarf · 🆕 Bow Tie · 🆕 Star Necklace · 🆕 Bandana | 2–4 |
| **Back** | 🆕 Jet Pack · 🆕 Fairy Wings · 🆕 Rock Guitar | 5–6 |

That's **17 items (13 new)**. Every item fits all 18 dino species, using the same head, eye, neck and back points as today's accessories. Back items sit behind the body. The growth-stage gear (backpack, compass, cape, medal) still shows.

The wardrobe screen has tabs for each spot and a big preview of the dino in its current outfit. Items owned before this change stay owned, and whatever the dino was wearing stays on.

## More crystals

### Skill gems (37)
**Every skill your child masters gives one gem**, in the colour of its world. The gems fill up that world's big crystal from the bottom, so the crystal shows how much of the world has been learned. A world crystal is still *won* the same way as today: beat the boss and reach 85%.
A small "✨ New gem!" message appears at the end of a mission when a gem is earned.

### Bonus crystals (8)
These are for good learning habits. Each shows a progress bar and is never random:

| Crystal | How to win it |
|---|---|
| Star Crystal | Score 60% in a full mock |
| Camp Crystal | Finish Training Camp 3 times |
| Never Give Up | "Never Give Up" badge 10 times |
| Explorer Crystal | 5 Real-World Missions |
| Sunshine Crystal | Play on 20 Adventure Days |
| Dino Friend | Find 10 dinos |
| Thinker Crystal | "Great Thinker" badge 10 times |
| ⭐ Rainbow Crystal | Win all 8 world crystals |

Each bonus crystal gives **+2 Dino Eggs**, so they also feed the wardrobe. There's no new currency: Stars and Eggs stay the only two.

### Crystal Cave screen
A new **Crystal Cave** page, reached from Home and the Adventure Map, shows the 8 world crystals filling with gems and the 8 bonus crystals. The 💎 counter on the map links to it.
