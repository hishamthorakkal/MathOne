Mathosaur – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Mathosaur is a maths practice game for young children preparing for the Class 2 Math Olympiad. We built it to collect nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no names, no email addresses, no location, no contacts, no photos and no device identifiers.

Where progress is saved

Game progress (stars, eggs, dinos, crystals, practice history and settings) is saved only on this device, in the app's local storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Grown-ups can delete it at any time from the Grown-ups settings ("Clear progress on this device").

Ads, tracking and third parties

There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party services. After the first launch the app works fully offline.

Read-aloud

The read-aloud button uses the text-to-speech voice built into the device. The text is spoken by the device itself.

Children

The app is made for children aged 5 to 8 and follows Google Play's Families policy. Because no personal information is collected, none is shared or sold.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see the contact email on the app's Google Play page.
